Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Job Assistant collects, why, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash) and, if you add it, your phone number.
  • Profile and resume: the details you add to your profile and the resumes you upload or edit.
  • Job search activity: your searches, the jobs you save, analyze or track, and your alerts.
  • Technical information: sign-in times, IP address and browser type, used for security.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: searching jobs, comparing them with your experience, tailoring your resume and sending alerts.
  • To keep accounts secure and the Service reliable, including enforcing usage limits and preventing abuse.
  • To send service emails such as verification codes and password resets. We don't send marketing without your consent.

Who can see it

Your resume, profile and applications are private to your account; other users can't see them. Our administrators can see account details and activity records (for example, sign-ins, search terms and the titles of jobs acted on) to operate the Service, provide support and prevent abuse. They do not view your resume content.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers for hosting, email delivery, job listings and automated analysis of job descriptions and resumes. They process information only on our instructions and may be located outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is open. Activity records are deleted after one year. When you delete your account, your profile, resumes, jobs and activity are permanently deleted.

Your rights

You can view and update your information in your profile and settings, download your resumes, and delete your account at any time. You may also request access to, or correction of, your personal information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, short-lived verification codes and strict separation of each user's data. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

Changes

We'll post changes here and, for significant ones, notify you by email.
