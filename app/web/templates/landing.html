What a Canadian resume looks like

Canadian employers expect a different resume than many other countries. Hirlumo checks yours for each of these.

  • No photo

    Canadian resumes leave out a photo, so nothing suggests bias in hiring.

  • No personal details

    Leave out date of birth, age, gender, marital status, religion and nationality.

  • No declaration or references

    No signed declaration, and references are given when the employer asks.

  • Dates as month and year

    "April 2021", not "04/05/21", which reads differently from country to country.

  • Canadian spelling

    A mix of British and American: colour, centre, travelled and licence, but organize and analyze.

  • One or two pages

    Most employers expect one or two; three only for a long senior or academic career. High school can come off.

  • City, province and phone

    Where you live in Canada and a number employers can call.

  • A summary, not an objective

    Two or three lines on what you offer, and education explained for Canadian readers.

  • Readable by ATS software

    One column, no tables or text boxes, contact details in the page itself, standard section headings.
