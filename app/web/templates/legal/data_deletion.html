Delete your data

You can delete your Hirlumo account and everything in it at any time. It takes effect right away and can't be undone.

Delete your account

  1. Sign in the way you usually do: email, Google, Facebook or LinkedIn.
  2. Open Account and scroll down to Delete account.
  3. Type your email address to confirm, then click Delete my account.

This permanently deletes your profile, resumes, saved and tracked jobs, tailored resumes, alerts, settings and activity, and the link between Hirlumo and your Google, Facebook or LinkedIn sign-in. We keep only one-way codes that can't be read back (made from your email, phone, name and resume text) and how many free-plan actions you used, so a new account doesn't reset the free plan's limits (see the Privacy Policy).

If you signed in with Facebook

Deleting your Hirlumo account as above removes everything we received from Facebook. To also remove Hirlumo from your Facebook account, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Hirlumo and click Remove. Removing it on Facebook only stops Facebook sharing with us; it doesn't delete your Hirlumo account, so do both.

The free resume check
