Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Hirlumo collects, why, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash) and, if you add it, your phone number.
  • Sign-in with Google, LinkedIn or Facebook: if you choose one, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from it, and from LinkedIn your country. We don't receive your password there, your contacts or your posts, and we never post for you.
  • Profile and resume: the details you add to your profile, including anything you import from a LinkedIn profile PDF or data export (read once to fill your profile, then discarded), and the resumes you upload or edit.
  • Job search activity: your searches, the jobs you save, analyze or track, and your alerts.
  • Feedback and messages: the star ratings and comments you give in a review, and the messages you send from the Contact us page, with your name and email address so we can reply. They're kept with your account and deleted when you delete it.
  • Help questions: the questions you type into the help assistant or the Help centre search, kept for 180 days without your name or account so we can improve the help.
  • Technical information: sign-in times, IP address and browser type, used for security.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: searching jobs, comparing them with your experience, tailoring your resume and sending alerts.
  • To email you the job alerts you turn on, as often as you choose. Every alert email has an unsubscribe link, and you can change how often you get them on your Watchlist.
  • To keep accounts secure and the Service reliable, including enforcing usage limits and preventing abuse.
  • To apply the free plan's limits per person, not per account. We keep one-way, keyed codes (hashes) made from the email addresses, phone numbers and name on your account and resumes, and from each resume's text. They can't be turned back into the information they came from; we only compare them, to see whether two accounts belong to the same person. If they do, those accounts share one free allowance.
  • To send service emails such as verification codes and password resets. We don't send marketing without your consent.

Who can see it

Your resume, profile and applications are private to your account; other users can't see them. Our administrators can see account details and activity records (for example, sign-ins, search terms and the titles of jobs acted on) to operate the Service, provide support and prevent abuse. They do not view your resume content.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers for hosting, email delivery, job listings and automated analysis of job descriptions, resumes and, when our own rules can't read it, the wording of a job search. They process information only on our instructions and may be located outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is open. Activity records are deleted after one year. When you delete your account, your profile, resumes, jobs and activity are permanently deleted. We keep only the one-way codes described above and the number of free-plan actions used, so that deleting an account and signing up again doesn't reset the free plan's limits. Nothing readable about you is kept.

Your rights

You can view and update your information in your profile and settings, download your resumes, and delete your account at any time (how to delete your data). You may also request access to, or correction of, your personal information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, short-lived verification codes and strict separation of each user's data. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

Changes

We'll post changes here and, for significant ones, notify you by email.
