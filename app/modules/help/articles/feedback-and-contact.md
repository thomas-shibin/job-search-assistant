## How do I contact Hirlumo?
?: talk to a human, speak to someone, customer support, support email, phone number, how long until you reply, response time, when will you answer
Use [Contact us](/contact) (in the account menu and at the bottom of every page). Pick a topic, add a title and your message, and select **Send message**. It's sent from your account's name and email address, and we reply by email, usually within two working days. Without an account, email {{ contact_email }}.

## How do I give feedback or rate Hirlumo?
After a few minutes of using Hirlumo, a short card asks how it's working for you: a few 1 to 5 star questions and an optional comment. Select **Send**, **Maybe later** (we ask again in a week) or **Don't ask again**. To rate it any time, select **Rate Hirlumo** in the account menu. We read every review.

## How do I report a problem?
Use [Contact us](/contact) and choose **Something isn't working**. Tell us the page, what you clicked and what happened; that helps us fix it quickly. See also [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).

## Can I ask for higher limits?
Yes. Choose **Higher limits** on [Contact us](/contact) and tell us what you're working on.
