## What is my profile for?
[My Profile](/profile) holds your details, experience, education and job preferences. A complete profile helps you find more relevant jobs. Only you can see it. **Profile strength** shows how complete it is and what's missing.

## How do I fill in my profile?
Open **My Profile** from the account menu. It has sections for **Personal information**, **Professional information** (headline, current title, years of experience, skills), **Job preferences** (preferred titles, locations, employment type and workplace), **Work experience** and **Education**. Select **Save** in each section. In Work experience, **Import from my resume** copies your roles from your uploaded resume.

## How do I import my profile from LinkedIn?
Under **Import from LinkedIn** on My Profile, choose one of:
- **Quickest, your profile PDF**: on your LinkedIn profile select **More** (or **Resources**), then **Save to PDF**. It lists your top 3 skills only.
- **Most complete, your LinkedIn data**: request a copy of your data on LinkedIn, choose **Want something in particular?** and tick Profile, Positions, Education and Skills. LinkedIn emails you when it's ready, usually within the hour.

Then select **Import** with the file (PDF, ZIP or CSV, up to 30 MB). Only what's missing is added; nothing you've entered changes. The file is read once and not kept.

## Is my profile the same as my resume?
No. Your resume on [My resume](/resume) is what matching and tailoring use. Your profile is your details and preferences. Each can fill the other: import roles from your resume into your profile, or upload a resume from the profile's **Resume** section.
