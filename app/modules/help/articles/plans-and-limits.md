Hirlumo is in beta, and everyone is on the **Free plan**. It includes everything Hirlumo does. The only limits are on job-board searches and on the features that cost us money each time they run. See every limit on [Plans](/plans).

## What does the free plan include?
?: how much does it cost, is it free, price, what do I get
Unlimited: searching company careers-site jobs, job pages, browsing by role and city, uploading and editing your resume with its full history, the Canadian resume check and converter, your profile and LinkedIn import, saved jobs, application tracking and job alerts.

Limited:
- **{{ free_searches_total }} job-board searches in total.**
- **{{ free_ai_actions_total }} resume actions in total**, and at most {{ ai_actions_per_hour }} an hour.

## What counts as a job-board search?
A new search that brings in job-board results counts once. These **don't** count: going to the next page of a search (up to {{ max_search_pages }} pages), changing filters, running the same search again within the hour, results Hirlumo already had, and searches where the job boards didn't answer. Each scheduled job-alert check that brings in job-board ads counts as one.

After {{ free_searches_total }}, your searches still work: they show company careers-site jobs only, and alerts stop checking the job boards.

## What is a resume action?
?: resume action meaning, what counts as an action
Anything that asks our analysis service to read your resume against a job: a **match analysis**, a **tailored resume** (including Quick Tailor and re-tailoring), **Canadian job title suggestions**, and reading a search the search box couldn't understand on its own. Each counts as 1. Answers already worked out for you (opening an analysis you ran before) don't count. Everything else in Hirlumo runs on our own rules and isn't counted.

## How do I see how much I have left?
?: how many left, resume actions left, searches left, remaining, check my usage, how many do I have, how many resume actions, how many searches
Open **Plan & usage** in the account menu (the [Plans](/plans) page): it shows what you've used and what's left. The results page also shows the job-board searches you have left, and job pages show your resume actions left.

## What happens when I run out?
?: limit reached, used all my searches, no searches left
Searches show company careers-site jobs only. Match analyses and tailored resumes stop, with a message. Everything else keeps working.

## Can I get more?
?: more searches, more resume actions, increase my limit, extend limit, need more
While Hirlumo is in beta, email {{ contact_email }} or use [Contact us](/contact) and tell us what you're working on.

## Can I make a second account to get more?
No. The free plan is one per person. Accounts that share an email address, phone number or resume share one allowance, and deleting an account and signing up again doesn't reset it.

## Will there be paid plans?
Not during the beta. If plans are added later, they'll be on the [Plans](/plans) page, and the limits there are always the current ones.
