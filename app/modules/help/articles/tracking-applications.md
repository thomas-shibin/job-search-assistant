## Where do I see the jobs I'm working on?
On [Applications](/applications) (account menu). It lists every job you've analyzed, tailored a resume for or applied to, best match first. Filter by **Status** or **Min score** and select **View** to open a job's page with its match analysis, tailored resume and next steps.

## How do I mark a job as applied?
Open the job's page and use the buttons under **Next steps**. They change with the job's stage:
1. A tailored resume ready for review: **Approve** or **Skip**.
2. Approved: **Open Application** opens the posting so you can apply.
3. After opening it: **Mark Applied**.
4. Applied: **Mark Interview**, **Mark Rejected** or **Archive**.
5. Interview: **Mark Offer**, **Mark Rejected** or **Archive**.

The job's status is shown at the top of its page and in the Applications list.

## What's the difference between Saved Jobs and Applications?
[Saved Jobs](/saved) is a bookmark list of jobs you want to look at again. A job moves onto [Applications](/applications) once you analyze it, tailor a resume for it or start applying.

## Are old jobs removed?
Jobs you never acted on are cleaned up after a while, with their scores and files, to keep your lists tidy. Jobs you have acted on, such as applying, are kept.
