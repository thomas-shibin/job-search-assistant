A **watch** is a saved search. Hirlumo checks for new jobs on a schedule and tells you about the ones that match, by email and, if you connect it, Telegram.

## How do I set up a job alert?
?: get notified of new jobs, email me new jobs, saved search, create alert
1. Open [Job Alerts](/watchlist) from the account menu.
2. Under **Add a watch**, give it a **Name** and fill in at least one of locations, titles, keywords or companies. Pick from the lists or type your own and press Enter. Within a box any entry can match; every box you fill in must match.
3. Optionally set **Alert when match score is at least** to hear only about jobs that fit your resume well, and under **More filters** a salary range, job type or how recently it was posted.
4. Select **Add watch**.

## How do I choose email or Telegram, and how often?
?: connect telegram, telegram setup, telegram bot
?: daily email, weekly email, notifications every day, how often alerts, stop emails, unsubscribe
On the same page, under **How you get alerts**:
- **Email alerts**: **After every scan**, **Daily summary**, **Weekly summary** or **Off**. Emails go to your account's address and every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Telegram**: select **Connect Telegram**, then **Start** in the chat that opens. **Send test message** checks it works; **Disconnect** stops it.

## How do I pause or delete an alert?
In the **Watches** list, select **Pause** (and later **Resume**), **Edit** or **Delete** next to the watch.

## Why didn't I get an alert for a job?
- The job didn't match every box you filled in, or scored below your minimum match score.
- You were already told about the same role (same company and title) in the last 30 days, so it was skipped as a duplicate.
- Email alerts are set to a daily or weekly summary, so it's waiting for your next email.

**Recent alerts** on the page lists every decision: sent, waiting for your next email, or skipped, and why.

## Do alerts use my free plan searches?
Yes. Each scheduled check that brings in job-board ads counts as one of your {{ free_searches_total }} job-board searches. Once they're used, alerts stop checking the job boards. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).
