## How do I see how well I match a job?
Select **Analyze match** on a job's details (in search results or on Saved Jobs). Hirlumo compares the job's requirements with your resume and opens the job's page with the result. You need a resume on [My resume](/resume) first.

## What does the match analysis show?
- A **fit score** out of 100 and a verdict.
- A short summary.
- **You match**: the requirements your resume shows.
- **Gaps**: what the job asks for that your resume doesn't show.
- A **Recommendation** on whether and how to apply.

## What is the keyword match?
A quick rule-based score from your resume's skills and experience against the job's words, shown under **Keyword match**. It's free and doesn't count toward your limits. The match analysis is the fuller comparison.

## Why does it say the analysis can't see the full requirements?
Job boards share only the start of a posting. Copy the whole description from the posting page, paste it under **Paste the full description** on the job's page and select **Save full description**. The keyword score updates, and the match analysis runs again if the job has one.

## How can I improve my match?
?: better match score, improve my score, raise my fit, low match, increase match
- **Look at the Gaps.** If you have that skill or experience but your resume doesn't say so, add it on [Edit resume](/resume/edit). Only add what's true.
- **Paste the full job description** if the job only has a preview, then run the analysis again.
- **Tailor your resume** for the job: it uses the job's language wherever your experience supports it. See [Tailored resumes](/help/tailored-resumes).
- **Check your resume was read correctly** on [My resume](/resume): anything missing there can't be matched.

## Does a match analysis count toward my limits?
Yes: each analysis is 1 resume action (you have {{ free_ai_actions_total }} on the free plan, and at most {{ ai_actions_per_hour }} an hour). Opening an analysis you already ran is free. **Re-run analysis** counts again. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## Where do I find jobs I've analyzed?
On [Applications](/applications), best match first. Filter by status or minimum score, and select **View** to open a job.
