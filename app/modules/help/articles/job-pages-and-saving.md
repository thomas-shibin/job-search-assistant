## How do I see a job's details?
Select **View Details** on any result. The details show the title, company, place, workplace, job type, salary if stated, when it was posted, and the description. On a phone the details open full screen; select **Back to results** to return.

## How do I apply for a job?
Select **Apply**. It opens the job's own posting, on the employer's careers site or the job board, where you apply. Hirlumo doesn't send applications for you. Before applying, you can run a [match analysis](/help/match-analysis) and get a [tailored resume](/help/tailored-resumes) for the job.

## Why is the job description cut short?
Job boards only share the start of each posting. Open the posting with **Apply** to read all of it. When you analyze a job, Hirlumo uses the full description wherever the company's careers site has it, and on the job's page you can **Paste the full description** for a more accurate analysis.

## How do I save a job?
Select **Save** on a result or in its details. It changes to **Saved**, and the job is on [Saved Jobs](/saved) (the bookmark icon at the top of the page, or the account menu).

## What can I do on Saved Jobs?
For each saved job: **Apply**, **Analyze & tailor** (a match analysis and a tailored resume in one step) or **Remove**. Jobs you analyze or tailor also appear on [Applications](/applications), where you track them.
