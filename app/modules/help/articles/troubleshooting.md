## My sign-in code or email didn't arrive
?: code never came, didn't get the code, no email received, verification code missing, confirmation email
Check your spam or junk folder, and that the address is typed correctly (select **Change** on the code screen to fix it). Wait a minute, then select **Resend code**. Codes are short-lived, so use the newest one.

## I can't sign in
Try signing in with an email code instead of a password, or use **Forgot password?**. If you signed up with Google, LinkedIn or Facebook, use the same button again. After several wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for a few minutes for your safety.

## My file was refused
Make sure it's your resume itself (not a cover letter or a scan), as a Word (.docx) or PDF file up to 10 MB. Hirlumo looks for parts like Experience, Education or Skills, with dates for each job. A PDF made from a scan or photo has no text to read; upload the Word version instead.

## My search found no jobs
?: search found nothing, no results, nothing found, search returns nothing, empty results
Use a broader title or fewer words, remove filters (a minimum salary hides jobs with no salary), try a nearby city or a distance, or check the spelling. Only jobs whose title matches your search are shown.

## It says the job board isn't responding
Job boards are sometimes busy. Results from company careers sites are still shown, and the search isn't counted. Try again in a minute.

## The results say they're from company careers sites only
?: why only company jobs, no job board results, only careers site jobs, fewer jobs than before
You've used the job-board searches included in the free plan, so searches show company careers-site jobs. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## It says I've reached a limit
There are two kinds: an hourly limit (wait until the time shown) and the free plan's total. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits) to check what's left, and [Contact us](/contact) to ask for more.

## A page looks wrong or a button does nothing
Reload the page. If it keeps happening, [send us a message](/contact) with the page and what you clicked.
