Search from [Find Jobs](/jobs). Results come from two places: job boards, and companies' own careers sites. Only jobs whose title matches what you searched are shown, the same job is shown once, and old ads are hidden.

## How do I search for jobs?
?: find jobs, look for work, job search, search two jobs at once, more than one title
Type a job title, keywords or a company in the first box, and a city, province or "Remote" in the place box, then select **Find Jobs**. You can search several titles or places at once. Separate them with commas, or write "and": "HR and Admin" (or "HR & Admin", "HR/Admin") searches HR jobs and admin jobs, and "Sales and Marketing Manager" searches sales managers and marketing managers.

## Can I type my search as a sentence?
Yes. Type something like "remote HR jobs in Toronto over 80k" or "entry level data analyst Calgary". Hirlumo reads it into titles, places and filters, shows **Showing results for** what it understood, and fixes spelling mistakes it's sure about. If it got it wrong, select **Search for the exact words instead**.

## How do I filter the results?
Select **Filters** and choose any of:
- **Workplace**: on-site, hybrid or remote.
- **Employment type**: full-time, part-time, contract and so on.
- **Experience level**.
- **Minimum salary**: shows only jobs that state a salary.
- **Date posted**.

Then select **Apply filters**. Each filter shows as a chip above the results; select it to remove it, or **Clear all**. Changing filters is free: it narrows what was already found and doesn't count as a new search.

## How do I search near a city?
?: jobs near me, within km, close to my home, commute
Type a town or city in the place box, then change **Exact location** next to it to a distance: **Within 10 km** up to **Within 100 km**. The search then covers every place within that distance. Distance only works from a town or city: a province is searched as a whole.

## Why do I see fewer results than I expected?
- Only jobs whose **title** matches your search are shown. The line above the results says how many less relevant jobs were hidden.
- Filters remove jobs that don't state what you filtered on (for example, a minimum salary hides jobs with no salary).
- Very old ads aren't shown.

Try a broader title, fewer filters, or a nearby city.

## Where do the jobs come from?
From **job boards** and from **companies' own careers sites**, which Hirlumo checks through the day. Careers-site jobs show a **Company career site** label. Job boards only share a preview of each posting, so open the posting to read all of it. See also [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits) for how job-board searches are counted.

## How many searches can I run?
- **Signed out**: company careers-site jobs only, with a small number of new searches each hour.
- **With an account**: up to {{ searches_per_hour }} new searches an hour. **{{ free_searches_total }} searches in total** also bring in job-board results on the free plan; after that, searches still work and show company careers-site jobs.

Going to the next page of results (up to {{ max_search_pages }} pages), changing filters, or running the same search again within the hour doesn't count. The results page shows how many job-board searches you have left.

## Can I browse jobs without searching?
Yes. [Browse jobs by role and city](/job-search) lists open roles by job type and by city, and [Companies](/companies) lists companies hiring in Canada with how many roles each has open. Both are open to everyone.
