## How do I change my name or phone number?
Open [Account & security](/account) from the account menu. Change them under **Profile** and select **Save profile**.

## How do I change my email address?
On [Account & security](/account), type the new address under **Email** and select **Change email**. We send a confirmation link to the new address; your email changes once you open it.

## How do I change or set my password?
On [Account & security](/account), use **Change password** (type your current one, then the new one twice). Changing it signs out your other devices. If you signed up with Google, LinkedIn or Facebook, you can **Set a password** to also sign in with your email.

## How do I sign out?
Open the account menu (your initials, top right) and select **Log Out**. To sign out everywhere else, select **Sign out other devices** on [Account & security](/account).

## How do I delete my account?

On [Account & security](/account), under **Delete account**, type your email to confirm and select **Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your account, resume, jobs, tailored resumes, alerts, settings, reviews and messages, and can't be undone. To stop the free plan being reset by signing up again, a coded record of the count is kept, which can't be read back into your details. See [Data deletion](/data-deletion).

## Who can see my resume and data?
Your resume, profile and applications are private to your account; other users can't see them. Hirlumo's administrators can see account details and activity records (such as sign-ins and search terms) to run the service, give support and prevent abuse; they don't view your resume content. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Where are my preferences?
[Settings](/settings) (account menu) holds your default job search, how jobs are scored, how tailored resumes are written, the addresses on them and your name on resumes. Changes apply right away.

## How do I switch to dark mode?
Use the sun or moon button at the top of the page (on a phone, in the menu): **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system**.
