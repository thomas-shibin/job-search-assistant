Your resume on [My resume](/resume) is your **base resume**: every match analysis compares jobs with it, and every tailored resume is made from it. Only you can see it.

## How do I upload my resume?
On [My resume](/resume), choose your file under **Upload your resume** (or **Replace your resume**) and select **Upload and use**. Word (.docx) and PDF files up to 10 MB work. The new resume is used right away for new analyses and tailored resumes; ones already made keep the version they were made from.

## Should I upload Word or PDF?
**Word (.docx) if you can.** Tailored resumes keep your Word file's exact design: fonts, layout and tables, with only the wording changed. A PDF can't be edited, so tailored resumes from a PDF get a plain standard layout.

## How do I check what Hirlumo read from my resume?
Under **What the app read from it**, My resume lists your contact line, summary, skills, each role with its bullets, education and certifications. Anything missing there can't be used for matching or tailoring. Text that didn't fit a section is listed under **Other text** and is still used.

## How do I edit my resume?
Select **Edit resume** on My resume. You can change your name and contact line, summary, skills, each role and its bullets, education, certifications and projects, add or remove roles, and select **Save resume**. Saving creates a new version; earlier ones are kept. Rewording or removing your summary and bullets is written straight into your Word file, keeping its design. A few changes can't be written into your design; the page tells you which, and you can save them with the standard layout instead.

## How do I see or undo earlier versions?
Every upload, edit and conversion is kept under **History** on My resume. For each version you can:
- **Compare** it with the version before it: changed, added, removed and moved lines are highlighted ([Compare versions](/resume/compare) lets you pick any two).
- **Download** it.
- **Use this version** to go back to it.

## Why was my file refused?
Hirlumo checks that the file is a resume: it looks for parts like Experience, Education or Skills, with dates for each job. If a file isn't recognised as a resume (for example a cover letter or a scanned image), it isn't used. Upload your resume itself, ideally as a Word file.

## Who can see my resume?
Only you. Its text is used only to analyze jobs and tailor resumes for you. See [Your account and privacy](/help/account-and-privacy).
