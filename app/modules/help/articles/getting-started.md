Hirlumo gets your resume ready for Canadian employers. It checks your resume against what Canadian employers expect and how applicant tracking software (ATS) reads it, fixes it in your own Word design, and writes a tailored copy for each job you apply to. It also finds jobs across Canada, from job boards and companies' own careers sites, and shows how well each one matches you.

## What can I do with Hirlumo?
?: what is hirlumo, what does this app do, features, how does it work
- **Check your resume for Canada**: a free check of your resume against Canadian conventions and how applicant tracking software reads it, with one-step fixes. See [The Canadian resume check](/help/canadian-resume-check).
- **Tailor your resume**: your own resume, reworded for one job, in your own Word design. See [Tailored resumes and Quick Tailor](/help/tailored-resumes).
- **See how well you match**: a match analysis for any job, with what you match and what's missing. See [Match analysis](/help/match-analysis).
- **Find jobs**: search by title and place, or type a sentence like "remote HR jobs in Toronto over 80k". See [Searching for jobs](/help/searching-for-jobs).
- **Keep track**: save jobs, track applications from applied to offer, and get job alerts by email or Telegram.

You can check your resume straight from the home page, without an account: choose your resume file under **Check your resume free**.

## How do I create an account?
1. Select **Sign Up** at the top of any page.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, **LinkedIn** or **Facebook**, or type your email address and select **Continue**.
3. With email, we send you a 6-digit code. Type it in and select **Verify**.
4. Add your first and last name and a password (at least 8 characters), then select **Create Account**.

Accounts are free. Signing up with Google, LinkedIn or Facebook only shares your name and email address (and your country, from LinkedIn); we never post anything for you.

## How do I sign in?
Select **Log In**, then either type your email and use the code we send you, or select **Sign in with password instead**. You can also use **Continue with Google**, **LinkedIn** or **Facebook** if that's how you signed up.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**, type your account's email and select **Send reset link**. The link in the email lets you choose a new password and expires in 1 hour. You can also sign in with an email code instead, without a password.

## Why do I need to confirm my email?
Until your email address is confirmed, a yellow notice at the top of the page says so and some features stay locked. Open the link we emailed you, or select **Resend the link** in that notice. Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived after a few minutes.

## What should I do first?
1. **Upload your resume** on [My resume](/resume). Every match analysis and tailored resume uses it. A Word file (.docx) is best: tailored resumes keep its exact design.
2. **Run the Canadian check** on [Canadian resume check](/resume/canadian) and make the fixes you want.
3. **Search for jobs** on [Find Jobs](/jobs), save the ones you like, and use **Analyze match** on the best ones.

After signing up you may see a short **Build your profile** step. It's optional: select **Continue Job Search** to skip it, and complete your profile any time from the account menu.

## Does Hirlumo write cover letters or apply for me?
?: cover letter, apply for me, send my application, auto apply, interview practice
Not at the moment. Hirlumo finds jobs, checks and tailors your resume, and helps you track applications, but it doesn't write cover letters or send applications for you: you apply on the employer's site with **Apply**. Ideas are welcome on [Contact us](/contact).

## Is there a Hirlumo mobile app?
?: iphone app, android app, app store, play store, download the app, phone app
Not yet. Hirlumo works in the web browser on your phone, tablet or computer: open hirlumo.com and sign in. On a phone, you can add it to your home screen from your browser's menu.

## Do I need an account to use Hirlumo?
?: without an account, without signing up, no account, use it without registering
No, for some things. Without an account you can search jobs from company careers sites, browse jobs by role and city, see the company list, and run the [free resume check](/canadian-resume-check). An account adds job-board results, saving and tracking jobs, alerts, match analysis and tailored resumes.
