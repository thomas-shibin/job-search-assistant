Canadian employers expect a resume without a photo or personal details, with clear dates, Canadian spelling and a sensible length. Many also use applicant tracking software (ATS) that can misread some layouts. The check looks at both and fixes what it can in one step.

## How do I check my resume for Canada?

- **With an account**: open [Canadian resume check](/resume/canadian) (or **Canadian check** on My resume). It checks your current resume.
- **Without an account**: use the [free resume check](/canadian-resume-check). Choose your PDF or Word file (up to 10 MB) and select **Check my resume**. No sign-up is needed to see your scores.

## What do the scores mean?
The free check gives two scores out of 100:
- **Canada-ready**: how well the resume follows what Canadian employers expect.
- **ATS readability**: whether tracking software is likely to read it correctly (tables, columns, text boxes, contact details hidden in the page header, and so on).

It also shows what the scores would be after the one-click fixes.

## What do must fix, should fix and tip mean?
Each finding has a level. **Must fix** items are things most Canadian employers reject or that break how software reads a resume, such as a photo or date of birth. **Should fix** items make a resume weaker, such as dates in an unclear format. **Tips** are worth considering but optional. Every finding says why it matters and what to do.

## How do I fix my resume?
On [Canadian resume check](/resume/canadian), each finding lists the exact changes. Tick the changes you want (or **Choose every suggested change**), then select **Make the chosen changes**. You see every change before it's made, and nothing is invented.
- **Word file**: the changes are made inside your own file, keeping your design.
- **PDF**: a new Word file is made in a standard one-column layout.

The result is saved as a new version and becomes your resume for matching and tailoring. Select **Download** to get it, **See what changed** to compare, or **Undo** to go back to the previous version.

## What about job titles that aren't common in Canada?
Under **Job titles**, you can add the Canadian equivalent in brackets after your title, for example "Senior Executive - HR (HR Generalist)". Your original title stays. **Suggest equivalents** proposes titles only where they're needed, never more senior than yours, and uses 1 resume action.

## Will the ATS check guarantee my resume gets through?
No tool can promise that: each tracking system is private and every employer sets its own filters. The check looks for what makers of these systems themselves document as breaking how a resume is read.

## What happens to the file I check without an account?
It isn't saved to disk or sent anywhere else. It's held in memory for 30 minutes, so you can create an account and fix it without uploading it again, then it's deleted. Select **No thanks, delete it** to delete it at once.
