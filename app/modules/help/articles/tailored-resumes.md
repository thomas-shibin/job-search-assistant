A tailored resume is your own resume with its wording adjusted for one job: the summary and bullets are rewritten to use the job's language where your experience supports it. Everything else (layout, fonts, skills section) stays exactly as in your base resume.

## How do I tailor my resume for a job?
?: change my resume for a job, adjust my cv for a job, make my resume fit a job, customise resume, resume for each application
On a job you've saved or analyzed, select **Analyze & tailor**. Hirlumo scores the job against your resume and, if it reaches your minimum score, writes a tailored copy. If the job is below your minimum score, select **Tailor anyway**. When it's ready, select **Download DOCX** or **Download PDF**.

## What is Quick Tailor?
?: job from another website, job I found elsewhere, paste a job description, linkedin job, indeed job
For a job you found somewhere else. Open [Quick Tailor](/quick) from the account menu, paste the job description, and select **Tailor my resume**. It uses your resume from My resume, or upload a different Word or PDF file (which becomes your new base resume). If the company or title is guessed wrong, set them under **Optional: job URL / override company / title**. Quick Tailor skips the scoring the job list uses.

## Will it make things up?
No. Your experience is never invented: every rewrite is checked against your original, and a rewrite that adds a claim is rejected and your original wording kept. The page lists how many rewrites were rejected.

## How do I see what changed?
Under **What changed in your resume**, each role shows its bullets **Before** and **After**: struck-out text was removed from your original, highlighted text is new wording. It also says how many bullets were kept exactly as written.

## Can I remove parts that don't matter for this job?
Yes. Hirlumo may suggest paragraphs that don't matter for this job. Tick **Remove from this resume** on any paragraph, then select **Apply removals**. Nothing is removed unless you choose it, and you can put it back the same way. This changes only this tailored copy, not your base resume.

## How do I tailor it again?
Select **Re-tailor** on the job's page, for example after updating your base resume or pasting the full job description.

## Does tailoring count toward my limits?
Yes: each tailored resume, Quick Tailor or re-tailor is 1 resume action. You have {{ free_ai_actions_total }} on the free plan, and at most {{ ai_actions_per_hour }} an hour. See [Plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).

## How do I change the minimum score or the address on my resumes?
On [Settings](/settings): **Matching** sets how jobs are scored, including the minimum score for tailoring, and **Address profiles** sets the address shown on tailored resumes.
